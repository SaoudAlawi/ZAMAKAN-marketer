We are pitching next month for **Sahm Pay (سهم باي)**, a payments and savings app for young Saudis (18–30).
They want a **Saudi National Day 2027 (23 September)** campaign that makes the app feel part of how young
Saudis celebrate, without the usual green-flag clichés.

- We need **10–15 strong references** for the pitch deck: proven or award-level work only, every link must work.
- Mix Saudi National Day / Founding Day work with the best fintech and youth-brand work from anywhere.
- End with 3–5 creative territories we can present, each with an Arabic name.
- **Avoid:** generic flag-waving, falcon and desert stock imagery, anything that looks like a government greeting.
