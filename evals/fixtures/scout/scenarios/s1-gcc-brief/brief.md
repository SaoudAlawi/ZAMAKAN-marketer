# Brief: Rawaq Coffee Roasters, Ramadan 2027

**Client:** Rawaq Coffee Roasters (رواق), specialty coffee, 9 cafés in Riyadh plus delivery apps
**Moment:** Ramadan 1448 (about 8 February to 9 March 2027) and Eid al-Fitr
**Objective:** Awareness and preference. Make Rawaq *the* place for suhoor gatherings and the after-taraweeh coffee run
**Audience:** Riyadh professionals aged 22–35, mixed groups of friends and families, who go out late during Ramadan
**Channels:** Snapchat, TikTok, Instagram (Reels and Stories), OOH near cafés, in-café experience
**Brand:** Warm, crafted, rooted. Najdi hospitality, the arch (riwaq) and the coffee bean. See the design system
**Competitors:** Barn's, Dose Café, Half Million, Overdose, Starbucks KSA
**Avoid:** Generic lanterns and crescents, camels at sunset, preachy tone, anything that feels like it trivialises worship
**Budget:** Mid-size, with no TV
**Deliverable from the scout:** References and 3–5 creative territories for the creative team's kickoff next week
