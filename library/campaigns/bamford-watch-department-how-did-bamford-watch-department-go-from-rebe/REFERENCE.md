# Bamford Watch Department: How did Bamford Watch Department go from rebel watch label to LVMH customiser?

> Bamford began in 2003 'to rediscover the lost art of personalisation in the age of mass production' and went from rebel to LVMH's official customiser.

## Insight

In a room of successful people, everyone wears the same watch: luxury had become a uniform.

## The idea

If you can imagine it, we can create it: personalisation as the true luxury.

## Execution

Blacked-out (DLC) Rolexes built a cult following; collaborations (Fragment, Dover Street Market) and official customiser deals with TAG Heuer, Zenith and Bulgari (per The Peak, 2018).
