# Roads and Transport Authority (RTA) Dubai: Dubai honours 638 taxi drivers by installing their names atop cabs

> #WhosYourCabbie: Dubai's RTA replaced the word TAXI on cab roof signs with the driver's own name, 638 drivers honoured for their work through the pandemic.

## Insight

Passengers spend time with a driver but rarely know his name; a name turns a service into a person.

## The idea

## Execution

RTA Dubai fitted 638 cabs with drivers' names on the roof lights (Gulf News, 2021); entered at Dubai Lynx and Cannes Lions.
