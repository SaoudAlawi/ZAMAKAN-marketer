## Insight

A large watch purchase is easier to justify as something for the family than as something for oneself.

## The idea

Reframe ownership as guardianship: the watch belongs to the next generation.

## Execution

Long-running print campaign of father-son and mother-daughter portraits by noted photographers; the 'Begin your own tradition' line later became 'You never actually own…' (per Hodinkee's history).

## Tone

Quiet, intimate, assured

## Visual devices

- parent and child
- candid black-and-white or soft light
- one line of copy
