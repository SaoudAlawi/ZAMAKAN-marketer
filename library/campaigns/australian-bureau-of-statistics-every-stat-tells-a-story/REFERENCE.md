## Insight

People know data informs roads and schools in general, but not that it shapes services in their own community (per the ABS statement).

## The idea

Every statistic is a story about real people, so tell the story, not the statistic.

## Execution

Integrated campaign by BMF and UM for Census 2021 (TV, radio, print, outdoor, digital, social), featuring real-life examples of how Census data is used; translated for multicultural communities and Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people.

## Channels

TV, radio, print, outdoor, digital, social
