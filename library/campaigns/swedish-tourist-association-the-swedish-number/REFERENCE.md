## Insight

## The idea

Give the nation a phone number that connects anyone in the world to a random Swede.

## Execution

The Swedish Tourist Association built one of the largest switchboards in the world with a contact-solutions partner, open 24/7; volunteers registered as ambassadors and the system picked one at random for each call (per the Ads of the World case page).
